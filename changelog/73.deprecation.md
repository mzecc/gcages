Renamed the `iamc` naming convention to `ar6_wg3` (both [gcages.renaming.SupportedNamingConventions][] and the corresponding column
of [gcages.databases.EMISSIONS_VARIABLES][]).
The old name was misleading: the mapping is specific to the IPCC AR6 WG3 database, not a generic IAMC convention.
**Warning**: `SupportedNamingConventions.IAMC` still works but raises a `FutureWarning` and will be removed in a future release.
Code reading the `iamc` column of `EMISSIONS_VARIABLES` directly must use `ar6_wg3`.
